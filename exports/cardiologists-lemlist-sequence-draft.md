## Cold-reader notes (2026-07-06)

- Read as a time-poor consultant: Email 1 passes because the first sentence is about THEIR patients'
  behaviour, not about Kobi. "Most consultants I look at barely appear" is honest and verifiable.
- Nigel placement in Email 1 (not held back): doctors trust peer proof above all else; leading with a
  named consultant cardiologist is worth more than any curiosity gap. Deliberate departure from the
  pro-services escalation.
- Subject A reads more personal; B reads more commercial. Recommend A. Neither uses "free" or
  "audit" (spam-adjacent in subjects).
- Audit is OFFERED not "already prepared" — we have no per-doctor data yet. If the Searchable
  audit generator gets built, flip Email 1 to the prepared-audit framing and re-run cold-reader.
- Risk flag: NHS mailboxes have aggressive filters; expect higher soft-bounce/no-open rates on
  nhs.net addresses. Lemlist open tracking will show this; consider deprioritising nhs.net
  addresses if deliverability suffers.

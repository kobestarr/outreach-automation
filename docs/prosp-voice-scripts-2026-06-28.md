# Prosp Voice-Note Scripts (record these)

The voice memo is **message 6** in the burst — a warm relationship opener *after* the
connect is accepted, **not a pitch**. Keep it 20-30s, smile while recording, say their
name once at the top, conversational (not read), no jargon, no calendar links.

Two ways to use these in Prosp:
- **AI voice clone** (scalable): record the 30s sample once (below), then each script is
  TTS'd in your cloned voice per lead with the merge var filled in.
- **Real broadcast recording** (warmer, recommended for high-status audiences): record one
  real MP3 per campaign — same memo to everyone in that campaign (no merge var, just "hey").

> House style: no dashes, no em dashes, warm, soft. The pitch comes later (human takeover in Kondo).

---

## Voice-clone training sample (record once, ~30-40s)
Read this aloud naturally to train the clone. Content is throwaway, it just captures your voice:

> "Hey, it's Kobi here. Really glad we connected. I spend most of my time helping businesses
> actually get found online, especially now that everyone's asking ChatGPT and Google's AI for
> recommendations instead of just searching. It's a fun problem to solve and the results can be
> pretty wild. Anyway, great to have you in my network, and I'm always happy to chat about what
> you're working on. Speak soon."

## Recording checklist
- [ ] Smile (it carries through the audio)
- [ ] Say {{first_name}} once, at the top
- [ ] 20-30 seconds, conversational pace
- [ ] No background noise, one take is fine with small imperfections
- [ ] No pitch, no calendar link, no jargon
- [ ] For the cardiologist/high-status waves: use a REAL recording, not the clone (it reads as AI on close listen)
